# Kandidat: Make the best flight game

## Kandidat 1: Neon Trench Run (Sci-Fi Atmospheric Canyon)
**Concept:** A high-speed low-altitude trench run through a neon-lit geometric canyon on an alien world, testing split-second banking and throttle control.
**Core Mechanics:** Afterburner heat gauge management, energy ring boosting, narrow canyon collision avoidance, progressive speed scaling.
**Visuals & HUD:** Chase view behind a sleek delta-wing fighter over glowing wireframe/shaded canyons. HUD features digital altitude tape, mach speed indicator, heading compass strip, and a vibrant green target ring ahead.
**Why it Wins:** Strong visual contrast and arcade immediacy that scores exceptionally high on visual polish and gameplay excitement under judge criteria 1, 3, and 4.

## Kandidat 2: Bush Pilot Mountain Pass (STOL Landing Simulator)
**Concept:** Bush flying through rugged mountain valleys at sunset, delivering vital cargo and mastering short-field landings on remote dirt airstrips.
**Core Mechanics:** Wind gust turbulence, stall warning aerodynamics, flap deployment, delicate touchdown flare and bounce physics.
**Visuals & HUD:** Cockpit dash view with glowing analog dials (artificial horizon, altimeter, airspeed) framed by windshield struts, looking out over rolling polygonal mountains toward a marked dirt runway. HUD overlays real-time speed, altitude, and heading bug.
**Why it Wins:** Perfectly aligns with the core prompt requirements (runway ahead, cockpit/chase view, instruments lit) while offering deep mechanical depth and landing realism (criterion 2 & 3).

## Kandidat 3: Aerobatic Airshow Stunt Circuit (Precision Flying)
**Concept:** Precision stunt flying through floating golden rings and checkpoint gates over a coastal bay at golden hour.
**Core Mechanics:** G-force fatigue meter, loop-and-roll momentum physics, time-trial scoring multiplier for clean center-ring passes.
**Visuals & HUD:** Smooth chase camera tracking a stunt aircraft over sunlit ocean water with sparkling reflection, glowing golden target rings, stunt HUD with G-meter, speed tape, heading, and altitude.
**Why it Wins:** Visually stunning coastal setting with clear target rings and immediate fun factor, maximizing accessibility and visual score.

## Kandidat 4: Stratospheric Glider (Thermal Updraft Soaring)
**Concept:** Atmospheric research glider navigating rising thermal updrafts inside a stylized low-poly thunderhead and cloud canyon.
**Core Mechanics:** Variometer lift/sink indicator, solar/battery charge management, thermal current soaring physics without engine power.
**Visuals & HUD:** Futuristic glass cockpit canopy view with holographic HUD projections, billowing volumetric clouds, floating wind-vector rings, altitude, vertical speed, and heading indicators.
**Why it Wins:** Highly original theme (gliding/thermals) and distinctive aesthetic that stands out in AI judge comparisons for originality (criterion 4).

## Kandidat 5: Carrier Night Ops (Jet Interceptor Approach)
**Concept:** Nighttime carrier-borne jet fighter landing and patrol mission under stormy seas with runway approach lights and instrument landing system.
**Core Mechanics:** Arrestor hook physics, angle-of-attack indexer, precision glide slope (meatball) guidance in turbulent night conditions.
**Visuals & HUD:** Cockpit HUD glowing green against dark stormy sea and horizon, lit aircraft carrier deck with flashing PAPI/meatball approach lights, altitude, airspeed tape, and compass heading.
**Why it Wins:** High atmospheric tension and technical landing mechanics that appeal heavily to simulation depth and polish criteria (criterion 2 & 3).

## Kandidat 6: Arctic Glacier Transport (Cargo & Survival)
**Concept:** Twin-engine cargo transport navigating whiteout arctic fjords, dropping supplies to remote weather stations on glacial shelves.
**Core Mechanics:** Wing icing hazard requiring de-icer activation, crosswind drift landing on snowy ice strips, fuel cargo weight distribution.
**Visuals & HUD:** Cockpit view with frost-edged windshield, snow-covered jagged polygonal mountains, glowing orange flare-marked runway ahead, digital HUD overlay with altimeter, true airspeed, and heading.
**Why it Wins:** Introduces compelling environmental survival mechanics (icing/weight) paired with a striking stark winter aesthetic.

## Kandidat 7: Solar-Sailor Stratosphere Explorer (Edge of Space)
**Concept:** High-altitude experimental solar aircraft flying at the edge of space over a curvature horizon.
**Core Mechanics:** Solar panel angle optimization relative to sun position, thin-air high-altitude stall dynamics, ultra-lightweight momentum.
**Visuals & HUD:** High chase view showing Earth's blue limb and black star-spangled stratosphere, futuristic sleek solar glider, telemetry HUD with solar power bar, altitude (meters/FL), airspeed, and heading.
**Why it Wins:** Breathtaking high-altitude sci-fi realism combined with an innovative solar energy management loop (criterion 2 & 4).

## Kandidat 8: Vintage Island Hop (1930s Mail Delivery)
**Concept:** Inter-island postal delivery service across a tropical archipelago in the 1930s using classic open-cockpit biplanes.
**Core Mechanics:** Carburetor mixture control, dead-reckoning compass navigation without GPS, fuel burn management, soft sand beach landing physics.
**Visuals & HUD:** Open-cockpit view with wooden dash, shaking analog airspeed and altimeter needles, tropical islands with palm trees and sandy beach runways, overhead compass tape and altitude readout.
**Why it Wins:** Evocative nostalgic exploration feel with authentic vintage aviation instruments and robust multi-island sandbox missions.

## Top 3
1. **Candidate 2 (Bush Pilot Mountain Pass)** — Best balance of authentic cockpit instruments, challenging landing mechanics, and direct adherence to all judge criteria.
2. **Candidate 1 (Neon Trench Run)** — Delivers exceptional visual impact, high-speed arcade excitement, and clear target/runway alignment.
3. **Candidate 5 (Carrier Night Ops)** — Outstanding atmospheric immersion and precision landing depth that scores high on feel and polish.
